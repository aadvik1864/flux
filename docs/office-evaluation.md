# Office integration decision — not yet selected

Evaluate ONLYOFFICE and appropriate alternatives such as Univer. Do not assume
that an open-source core grants every required embedding, hosting, collaboration,
or import/export capability for the intended deployment.

The user requires **self-hosted/free** and no purchases. The 2026-10-12 evidence
review found licensed Univer collaboration and AGPLv3/additional terms for
ONLYOFFICE Community. Neither is approved for Flux's intended packaging yet;
see [integration feasibility](integration-feasibility.md) for primary sources.
No Office service or accepted co-editing prototype exists. Do not substitute a
time-limited trial or silently buy a Developer/commercial license.

semantic Office merging or exact fidelity for arbitrary complex Office files.
